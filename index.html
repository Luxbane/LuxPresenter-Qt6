Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LuxPresenter.

Is LuxPresenter truly 100% free?

Yes! LuxPresenter is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0). There are no trial periods, no watermarks, no locked features, and no monthly fees. It is completely free for every church, ministry, and event.

Can it run on a 1366x768 resolution laptop?

Absolutely. The application's minimum window size is 750x460 with flexible drag-and-drop splitters, meaning it fits comfortably on standard 1366x768 laptop screens without crowding your controls.

What is Input Guard (Typo Protection)?

Input Guard is our built-in safety feature that actively validates every key you press in real time against your song database. If you accidentally hit an erroneous letter or double space, the app ignores the invalid key, keeping your search clean and preventing embarrassing presentation mistakes on stage.

Does LuxPresenter require an internet connection on Sunday?

No. LuxPresenter is completely offline-first. All your songs, Bibles, media, and presentation slides are stored locally on your PC. An internet connection is only needed if you wish to use the embedded web browser tab.
